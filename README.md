### Hi, I'm Abel González Mondéjar 👋

PhD in Computer Science (PUC-Rio, 2024). I build AI for digital health: digital phenotyping, physiological time series and software as a medical device (SaMD).

- 🧠 Co-founder & CEO of [BrainHeal](https://brainheal.com.br): BrainBridge gives psychologists and psychiatrists continuous patient context collected through two companion apps (BrainHeal Meu and BrainHeal Vínculo). It grew out of my PhD research (BraPolar, PUC-Rio / IPUB-UFRJ); BraPolar2 is registered at INPI (BR 51 2024 002657-6).
- 🛠 Senior Systems Analyst at the [Tecgraf Institute / PUC-Rio](https://www.tecgraf.puc-rio.br/), leading applied R&D for Petrobras: Infra360 (digital planning of temporary infrastructure for offshore maintenance campaigns: 3D computational geometry, point clouds, computer vision, combinatorial optimization) and, before that, SuiteVR (real-time physiological monitoring of offshore divers).
- 📚 Publications in the Journal of Biomedical Informatics, IJQRM, IEEE SeGAH, IEEE CBMS, HEALTHINF and Springer LNCS: [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7111-5444) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=rDQEpWwAAAAJ) · [Lattes](https://lattes.cnpq.br/9188829682223084)
- 👯 Open to research collaborations, clinical partnerships and applied ML work in digital health.
